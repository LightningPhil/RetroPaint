START-001 · L1 · P1 · Not run

Find the paper and make a first mark

As a beginning young artist, I want to find the paper and make a first mark, so I can learn by trying things while keeping control of my picture.

Workflow and expected results

Fixture: PLAY. Ready painting paper.

  1. Open a fresh session without a saved preference
  2. Choose the illustrated Brushes tool and a bright colour
  3. Drag once across the paper
Acceptance criteria
  • Paper is ready without an account or mandatory tutorial
  • The tool and chosen colour are visibly selected
  • A single gesture produces a mark and enables Undo

Failure path: A returning recovery prompt must leave the ready canvas usable until the user chooses to resume.

  2. Press Escape before the gradient is committed

Acceptance criteria
